Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

1. Who uses the app

Shorts Studio is a personal tool used only by the owner of the TikTok channel "The Mystery Logs". It has no other users and collects no data from the public or from visitors of this page.

2. Data accessed

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token for the owner's own account. It is used only to upload the owner's videos. The app does not read, collect or store information about other TikTok users.

3. Storage

The access token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party.

4. Retention and deletion

The owner can delete the stored token at any time and can revoke the app's access in the TikTok account settings.

5. Contact

Questions can be sent through the GitHub profile that hosts this page: github.com/mystery-logs-media.
